Medication Safety & Educational Reference Guide

Essential patient safety rules, drug-food interactions, and verified medication facts.

1. Prescribed Dosage Only

Never increase, reduce, or stop a dosage without consulting your physician. Dosing is tailored to your weight, renal function, and clinical status.

2. Never Double Dose

If you miss a dose, take it as soon as remembered unless it is almost time for your next scheduled dose. Never take two doses together.

3. Meal Timing Adherence

"Before Food" protects absorption rate (e.g. Levothyroxine); "With / After Food" shields your gastric lining against irritation (e.g. NSAIDs, Metformin).

4. Complete Antimicrobial Regimens

Always complete your full prescribed course of antibiotics even if feeling better, to prevent bacterial resistance and recurrence.

⚠️ Critical Interaction Alerts to Know

Medication Class Avoid Combining With Potential Clinical Risk
NSAIDs (e.g. Ibuprofen, Naproxen) Blood thinners (Warfarin, Aspirin), Oral Steroids Severe gastrointestinal bleeding and kidney impairment.
Statins (e.g. Atorvastatin, Simvastatin) Grapefruit and Grapefruit juice Inhibits CYP3A4 enzyme, leading to toxic drug buildup and muscle breakdown (rhabdomyolysis).
ACE Inhibitors (e.g. Lisinopril, Enalapril) Potassium supplements, Salt substitutes High risk of hyperkalemia (dangerous cardiac arrhythmias).
Antibiotics & Sedatives Alcoholic beverages Excessive CNS depression, liver toxicity, or severe disulfiram-like nausea reactions.
